\section*{\textbf{Cover Letter}}

\noindent\textbf{Dear Editors and Reviewers,}

We sincerely thank the reviewer for the careful assessment of our manuscript, entitled ``A Lightweight Four-Credential Authentication and Key Agreement Protocol for AI-Enabled Edge-IIoT: Transcript-Bound Resistance to Replay and Impersonation.'' The comments were constructive and helped us improve the precision and reproducibility of the presentation.

For this independent review round, we made five focused revisions. We now define protocol fields as the leftmost 160 bits of SHA-256 while retaining full-SHA-256 host timing; correct the distinction between a single CEAF guess, the online union bound, and the ROR advantage term; label Table~5 as provisional deployment guidance; identify the supplementary benchmark, fuzzy-reproduction routine, HLPSL model, AVISPA report, and public repository; and mark qualified entries in Table~3 with an explicit ``P'' notation.

The response letter provides a point-by-point reply to the current Reviewer~1 comments. We have retained the manuscript's stated limitations and have not converted analytical or bounded-model results into implementation or usability claims.

We hope that the revised manuscript and the detailed responses satisfactorily address the reviewers' concerns and are suitable for consideration in the themed collection.

\vspace{3em}

\noindent Sincerely,

\noindent Xun Zhang, Zhiying Mu, Wu Wei, Zhihui Mu, Dejun Mu, and Xin Liu
